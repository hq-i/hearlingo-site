HearLingo Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Last updated: 2026-09-22 · 最后更新：2026-09-22

What we collect / 我们收集什么

Nothing. 什么都不收集。 The extension contains no analytics, no tracking, no advertising SDKs, and does not transmit any personal data to HearLingo developers. 本扩展不含分析统计、跟踪或广告组件，不向开发者传输任何个人数据。

Local storage / 本地存储

Settings and caches are stored locally on your device. Remove everything by uninstalling the extension and deleting the companion app data folder. 设置与缓存存储在你的设备本地。卸载扩展并删除伴侣程序数据文件夹即可全部清除。

Companion app (Windows) / 伴侣程序

The optional companion app runs a local service on 127.0.0.1 (your machine only). It bundles the OCR engine and performs speech synthesis and translation requests on behalf of the extension. 可选的伴侣程序在本机 127.0.0.1 上运行本地服务（仅本机可访问），内含 OCR 引擎， 代表扩展执行语音合成与翻译请求。

They may be inaccurate, incomplete, or wrong, and their output is provided for reference only — it is not professional translation and should not be relied upon for legal, medical, financial, or other consequential decisions. 译听为辅助工具。语音识别、机器翻译与语音合成由第三方服务和本地模型完成， 可能不准确、不完整或有误，输出仅供参考——不构成专业翻译，不应作为法律、医疗、 财务等重大决策的依据。

The free endpoints and models used by HearLingo are operated by third parties. Their availability, rate limits, response quality, and continued existence are outside HearLingo's control and may change or be discontinued at any time without notice. HearLingo is not affiliated with these providers. 译听使用的免费接口与模型由第三方运营，其可用性、限流、响应质量与持续存在 不受译听控制，可能随时变更或停止。译听与这些服务商无隶属关系。

Reading timeliness also depends on your network connection, third-party service response speed, and your computer's performance. 朗读的及时性还取决于您的网络连接、第三方服务响应速度与电脑性能。
